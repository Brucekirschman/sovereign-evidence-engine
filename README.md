### How to run

1. Download `see-0.0.24aadz.html`
2. Disconnect from internet if you want.
3. Open s.e.e.
4. Type your "receipt" in the left column. Add your "evidence" in the right column with OPEN button. "I saw this then." Type your witness. Activate phone's GPS sensors to include your location coord lat/long. Show your evidence on the spot with camera picker, stills, vid, add files .txt .doc .pdf etc. all will appear in right column as thumbnails.
5. Click MINT to create a zip file of your bundled evidence. Includes a timestamped signature SHA-512 hash-fingerprinted .json manifest. Your receipt is automatically generated in chronological order as a webpage timeline that may be run locally stand-alone offline and/or uploaded.
6. Take your freshly minted zip file and save it in as many places as you can for legal security.
7. WIPE. Do it again. OPEN your last zip file to chain.
